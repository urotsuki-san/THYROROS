# Code of conduct

Participate in good faith. Discuss code, evidence, threat assumptions, and trade-offs without
harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, or disclosure of another person's private
information.

Security reports must use the private process in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove
content that endangers users, exposes secrets, or prevents constructive technical collaboration, and
may restrict participation after repeated or serious violations.

Questions about moderation may be raised privately with the repository owner through their GitHub
profile.
